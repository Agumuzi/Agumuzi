02 / PHILOSOPHY

How I build.

我的开发原则。

01

Local-first

Keep data and essential workflows under the user's control whenever practical.

尽可能把数据与核心工作流保留在用户自己控制的设备上。

02

Modular

Design replaceable components and boundaries that make failures easier to isolate.

清晰划分模块，让组件可替换、问题可定位、维护更简单。

03

Real-world use

Build, test, document limitations, and keep improving from actual workflows.

从真实场景出发，持续测试、记录限制，并逐步迭代。
